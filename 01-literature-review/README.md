## Workflow

1. Record the confirmed base paper and official link in `base-paper/`.
2. Assign papers in the master sheet; give every paper a stable ID such as P01.
3. Fill paper title, authors, year/venue, method, dataset, metrics, limitations, relevance, reviewer and status.
4. Optional longer notes go under `member-reviews/<name>/P01-short-title.md`.
5. Compare findings in `synthesis/`; link evidence when proposing a gap.
6. Export a dated `.xlsx` snapshot after major updates to `spreadsheet-snapshots/`, e.g. `literature-review-2026-10-21.xlsx`.
